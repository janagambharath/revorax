FAQ

Asked by every PG owner.

My list is in a notebook / on paper. Will this work?

Yes — that's exactly what the photo upload is for. Snap the pages, we read the names and numbers, you verify once. No typing the whole list.

Do tenants need to install an app?

No. Reminders arrive as ordinary WhatsApp messages with a pay link. Nothing to install, nothing to learn.

What if a tenant doesn't have WhatsApp?

We fall back to SMS for those numbers automatically. Same reminder, same pay link.

I already use a PG management app. Why do I need this?

Most apps track rent — they don't collect it. Revorax is the collection layer: automatic reminders with pay links, plus a simple paid/unpaid/overdue view. It works alongside whatever you use.

What happens after the 2-week trial?

If your collections improved, it's ₹799/month. If not, we switch everything off — you keep your list, no charge, no hard feelings.
