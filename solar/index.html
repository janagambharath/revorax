First Line Sales for Solar

The first conversation,
handled before sales starts.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't replace your sales team.
  • It doesn't negotiate your final price.
  • It doesn't conduct the site survey.
  • It doesn't replace the relationship with the customer.
  • It doesn't decide how you close the deal.

What it does do

  • It calls within seconds of a form submission — before they contact the next solar company.
  • It has the first conversation while the enquiry is fresh — not sitting in a CRM waiting for a salesperson.
  • It asks the first qualification questions, so your sales team skips the basic-details round.
  • It labels every lead by intent — High Intent, Callback, Info Needed, Not Interested, Wrong Number.
  • It records and transcribes every call — your salesperson gets full context before ever speaking to the customer.
  • It extracts what matters — requirements, location, budget, timeline, and qualification details.
  • It hands the qualified opportunity to sales with the conversation attached — context first, not "hello, calling regarding your enquiry."

By the time your salesperson gets involved, they aren't calling a lead. They are following up on an opportunity.
