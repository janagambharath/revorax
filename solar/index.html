FAQ

Which languages does the voice agent speak?

Telugu, Hindi and English — spoken natively by the voice model, not machine-translated. The agent picks the prospect's language automatically.

How fast does it call a new lead?

Seconds after the enquiry arrives — from Meta Lead Ads, your website form, or any CRM webhook. No lead waits hours for a callback.

Does it replace my sales team?

No. It handles instant follow-up and qualification — the repetitive part. Your team talks only to qualified prospects who want a site survey.

What happens after the call?

The full transcript lands on your dashboard with extracted details — house type, roof space, monthly bill — synced to your pipeline, plus WhatsApp follow-up.
