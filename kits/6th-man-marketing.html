First Line Front Desk for Med Spas

The first conversation, handled before the front desk starts.

What it doesn't do

  • ✕It doesn't replace the front desk team.
  • ✕It doesn't give medical advice or diagnose anything.
  • ✕It doesn't decide treatments or prices.
  • ✕It doesn't replace the relationship with the patient.
  • ✕It doesn't decide how the practice runs its day.

What it does do

  • ✓It answers on the first ring, 24/7 — before the caller tries the next med spa.
  • ✓It has the first conversation while the inquiry is fresh — not sitting in voicemail waiting for a callback.
  • ✓It asks the first questions, so the team skips the basic-details round — new or returning, treatment of interest, preferred time.
  • ✓It labels every inquiry — Booked, Callback, Info Needed, Not Interested, Wrong Number.
  • ✓It records and transcribes every call — the team gets full context before speaking to the patient.
  • ✓It extracts what matters — treatment, preferred provider, timeline, and contact details.
  • ✓It hands the booked appointment to the team with the conversation attached — context first, not “hi, returning your call.”

By the time your client's team gets involved, they aren't returning a missed call. They are welcoming a booked patient.
