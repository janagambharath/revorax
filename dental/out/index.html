AI Voice Agent for Dental Clinics

The first conversation,
handled before your receptionist starts.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't replace your receptionist.
  • It doesn't diagnose or prescribe anything.
  • It doesn't quote treatment prices.
  • It doesn't replace the doctor-patient relationship.
  • It doesn't decide clinical treatment.

What it does do

  • It calls back every missed call in seconds — before the patient calls the next clinic.
  • It books the appointment directly — day and time confirmed on the call.
  • It calls the day before to confirm — so your chairs don't sit empty on no-shows.
  • It sends the clinic address and appointment details on WhatsApp.
  • It records and transcribes every call — your front desk gets full context before the patient walks in.
  • It extracts what matters — concern, slot, confirmation status, callback requests.
  • It hands the booked patient to your receptionist with the conversation attached — context first, not "hello, calling regarding your enquiry."

By the time your receptionist gets involved, she isn't chasing a missed call. She is welcoming a booked patient.
