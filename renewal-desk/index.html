FAQ

Questions gym owners usually ask.

Prefer a straight answer? Message us and we’ll walk through your current setup.

WhatsApp us →
Do I need a new biometric machine?+

No. Renewal Desk can connect to compatible eSSL and ZKTeco devices already installed at the gym through the Windows bridge.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?+

Yes. Each gym connects its own WhatsApp Business setup so members receive messages from the right gym number.

What happens when someone renews?+

After payment is verified, their membership becomes active, renewal reminders stop, and compatible biometric access can be restored.

What if a member already paid?+

The reminder logic checks recent verified payments so members are not sent unnecessary renewal reminders.

What if the internet goes down?+

The exact behavior depends on your installed device and PC setup. We will confirm it during your demo rather than promise an offline access state we cannot verify.

Is my data shared with another gym?+

No. Each gym’s members, payments and staff access are kept separate.

Does it work with every biometric machine?+

No. Renewal Desk currently supports compatible eSSL and ZKTeco setups through the Windows bridge.
