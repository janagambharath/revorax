How it works

Three things that actually run your renewals.

Not 40 features you'll never use. Three mechanics that close the gap between "membership expired" and "money in your account."

Every membership tracked to the day

Add members once, set their plan and dates. The dashboard shows you exactly who's expiring this week, who's overdue, and who's active — at a glance, per gym.

WhatsApp reminders go out automatically

Every day, the system scans for upcoming and overdue expirations and sends personalized WhatsApp messages — with your gym's payment QR code attached so members can pay right from the chat.

Smart skip: If a member already paid or has a verified payment in the last 7 days, they don't get reminded. No embarrassing duplicate messages.

The door locks itself

Connected to the same eSSL or ZKTeco fingerprint scanner you already have at your entrance. When a membership lapses, that member's access turns off automatically. When they renew, it turns back on.

No awkward front-desk confrontation. No manual work for your staff. No more "membership khatam ho gaya but door abhi bhi khulta hai."

The real differentiator

Your biometric scanner finally knows who's paid.

Most gym software has no idea your biometric door exists. Renewal Desk connects to it through a small Windows bridge app running on your gym's PC — the same PC your eSSL/ZKTeco device is already plugged into.

When a membership expires, a disable command goes to the device. That member's fingerprint or face no longer opens the door. Period.

When they renew and you verify the payment, an enable command goes out instantly. Access restored. No one at the front desk had to do anything.

Payment verification

Confirm the payment. Everything else updates itself.

This is India — members pay via UPI, Google Pay, or cash at the counter. No complex payment gateway needed. When a member pays, your staff opens Renewal Desk and marks the payment as verified. That's it.
