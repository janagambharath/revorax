What it doesn't do

  • ✕It doesn't replace your team.
  • ✕It doesn't negotiate your final price.
  • ✕It doesn't diagnose or decide treatment.
  • ✕It doesn't replace the relationship with the customer.
  • ✕It doesn't decide how you close the deal.

What it does do

  • ✓It answers — or calls back — within seconds of the enquiry, before they contact the next business.
  • ✓It has the first conversation while the enquiry is fresh — not sitting in a CRM or voicemail.
  • ✓It asks the first qualification questions, so your team skips the basic-details round.
  • ✓It labels every lead by intent — Booked, High Intent, Callback, Info Needed, Not Interested, Wrong Number.
  • ✓It records and transcribes every call — your team gets full context before speaking to the customer.
  • ✓It extracts what matters — requirements, location, timeline, and contact details.
  • ✓It hands the qualified opportunity to your team with the conversation attached.

By the time your team gets involved, they aren't chasing a cold enquiry. They are following up on an opportunity.
